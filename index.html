Line 2 on the night shift uses about 9% more material than the recipe when making Coil Ø32

Since July, 12 batches on this line, shift and product used more resin and masterbatch than the standard recipe allows. Every other combination of line, shift and product stays within 0.1% of the recipe, so this is one specific place to look, not a general problem.

  • 12batches affected, out of 887
  • 1.1 tonnesmore material than the recipe allows
  • IDR 34 millionof extra material cost

Worth asking: what is different about that line, shift and product since July? A machine setting, resin handling, scrap that is not written down, or a recipe that is out of date. The extra material is not showing up as extra pipe or recorded scrap, so it is unaccounted for.

PP-R resin and Ice-blue masterbatch are going missing: the counted stock is below the records

Each month the warehouse counts what is physically there. After taking out the duplicate entries, PP-R resin and ice-blue masterbatch fall short of the records, and the gap has widened at every count since July. The other 2 materials reconcile within normal counting error.

  • 1,805 kgof PP-R resin missing at the September count, worth IDR 53 million. Short since July.
  • 602 kgof ice-blue masterbatch missing at the September count, worth IDR 28 million. Short since July.
  • 0.2%the largest gap on any of the other 2 materials, as a share of a month's use. That is normal counting error.

Worth asking: where does ice-blue masterbatch go after it leaves the store room? The gap grows by roughly 6% of a month's use each month, which a one-off counting error would not do. It points to unrecorded use, waste, or material leaving the site.

CV Sumber Plastik charged 10% to 17% more than other suppliers for the same resin

3 of 4 PP-R resin orders from CV Sumber Plastik were priced well above what the other suppliers charged in the same weeks. Resin prices rose about 7% across the other suppliers from August, and the check allows for that by comparing each order with prices from other suppliers at the same time.

  • 3 orderspriced above other suppliers, all from CV Sumber Plastik
  • IDR 201 millionpaid above the other suppliers' price on those orders
  • 10% to 17%above the other suppliers' price

Worth asking: is there a reason, such as a better grade, faster delivery or longer credit? If not, those orders can move to the cheaper suppliers. The same check runs on every material automatically.

Material margin slipped by 3 to 6 points on every pipe size between April and September

The average price paid for PP-R resin, the largest ingredient, rose 10% from April to September, while PE-RT resin stayed flat, but selling prices barely moved (-0.2% on Coil Ø20). On top of that, one customer paid about 13% less than the usual price for Straight Ø40 from August.

    Worth asking: should selling prices follow resin cost, and who approved the extra discount? Material margin is selling price less material cost only, so labour, power and overhead come on top and the real margin is lower. Coil Ø16 is thin from the start, at about 17% now.

    How this was produced

    1. 1. ReadThe five spreadsheets, as they are, read-only. Nothing is changed in the originals.
    2. 2. CleanSpellings, dates, units and duplicates are fixed in one database, and every fix is logged.
    3. 3. CheckSix automatic checks compare records with each other: recipe against use, books against counts, price against price, and so on.
    4. 4. ReportThis page and an Excel exception list, rebuilt on demand, with every flagged row traceable to its source row.

    What to keep in mind. This is made-up data, built so the problems could be found. Real records will hold different problems, some of them harder to see. Each flag is a question to ask on the factory floor, not a verdict: a higher supplier price may buy a better grade, and extra material use may have a reason nobody wrote down. The thresholds (3% over recipe, 10% over peer price, 0.4% of a month's use for stock) are settings that should be agreed with the factory.
